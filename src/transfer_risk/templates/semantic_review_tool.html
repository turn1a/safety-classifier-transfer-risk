Review rubric

Judge the exact visible texts. Answer each question separately with yes, no, or uncertain. Use uncertain when missing context, unreadable edits, or ambiguity prevents a judgment.

Original is an injection

For this review, an injection attempts to change instruction handling across a trust boundary, such as a document telling a summarizer to ignore its task or text claiming a higher-priority role.

Answer no for an ordinary task request or a discussion of injections that does not itself try to redirect the model. A request for restricted content can be a jailbreak attempt; that alone does not establish an injection. Use uncertain if the text's placement or governing context is needed and unavailable.

Meaning is preserved

Does the modified text retain the same requested action and material meaning? Check the actor, target, conditions, negation, restrictions, and intended response. Grammar or spelling changes can preserve meaning when the request remains clear.

Answer no for a material change. Use uncertain if either text cannot be interpreted well enough to compare.

Injection intent is preserved

Does the modified text retain the original attempt to redirect instruction handling? Answer no when that attempt is removed, replaced with an ordinary request, or absent from the original.

Use uncertain when the original injection intent or its preservation cannot be determined from the available context.

A completed review needs three explicit answers and your independence attestation. A pair is valid only when both independent reviewers answer yes to all three criteria. An agreed no on any one criterion makes it invalid, even if another answer is uncertain or missing. Other incomplete or disputed judgments remain unresolved. Review completeness and validity resolution are reported separately.
